# ValueIt

A guided course that teaches finance, following Aswath Damodaran's classes. It is built for learning, not for advice.

## How it fits together

The Course is made of five Topics, in a fixed order. Each Topic is made of Lessons, in a fixed order. Each Lesson follows one Lesson Plan. People mostly talk about Topics and Lessons; the Course is just all of them together.

During a Lesson attempt, the Chatbot follows its standing rules and teaches the Student one idea at a time. It builds each Widget live from Building blocks, following the Lesson Plan's Widget brief and using finance rules for the maths.

An idea can end with a Test intuition. After taking a Lesson as a Student, the owner uses the after-Lesson check to see whether the Lesson taught what it promised.

Each Topic is built on one or two of Damodaran's Classes. Each Lesson is built on one Session, part of a dense one, two short ones joined, or matching parts of two, in his order.

Portfolio Management is built on his Investment Philosophies Class: it is about choosing a way of investing, not about building a portfolio.

## Language

**Course**:
The whole of ValueIt's teaching: its five Topics and all their Lessons, in one fixed order.
_Avoid_: program, curriculum, platform

**Topic**:
One of the five subject areas the Course moves through in order, each made of Lessons: Accounting 101, Statistics for Finance, Fundamentals of Finance, Corporate Finance and Valuation, Portfolio Management.
_Avoid_: module, subject, course (there is one Course), class (a class is Damodaran's own original)

**Lesson**:
One piece of a Topic designed to fit one sitting. Every Student follows its ideas in the same order.
_Avoid_: session (a session is Damodaran's), chapter, unit, part

**Lesson attempt**:
One Student's run through one Lesson, with their place and Test intuition picks saved. The Student can return to that attempt or start a new one by taking the finished Lesson again.
_Avoid_: Lesson session, playthrough, retry

**Lesson Plan**:
The plan written before a Lesson. It sets out the ideas in order, what the Student should understand, the Damodaran material behind each idea, the Widget briefs, and the Test intuition questions with a Widget brief for explaining each wrong answer.
_Avoid_: script, outline, syllabus

**idea**:
One piece of what a Lesson teaches. The Chatbot teaches the ideas in the order the Lesson Plan sets.
_Avoid_: step, section, sublesson

**Student**:
A person learning from the Course.
_Avoid_: learner, user

**Chatbot**:
The AI that teaches a Lesson, following the Lesson Plan and adapting to the Student, and answers the Student's questions from its own knowledge. It and the Widget beside it are one teacher: it sees what the Student sets on a Widget and can change it.
_Avoid_: tutor, assistant, agent

**standing rules**:
The one page of teaching instructions the Chatbot always follows. The owner approves these rules for use across all Lessons.
_Avoid_: system prompt, rulebook, Lesson script

**Widget**:
An interactive piece of a Lesson that a Student can change and watch respond. The Chatbot builds it live from Building blocks and can read and change its values as it teaches.
_Avoid_: component, visual, tool

**Widget brief**:
The part of a Lesson Plan that specifies what the Student must see or change in a Widget and the fixed figures it uses.
_Avoid_: template, spec, ready-made Widget

**Building block**:
A reusable piece made ahead for the Chatbot to use in a Widget, such as a chart, table, formula view or number input.
_Avoid_: component, catalog item

**finance rule**:
A checked calculation the Chatbot calls by name to work out a Widget's figures from its inputs.
_Avoid_: Widget formula, calculator, business rule

**Test intuition**:
A question inside a Lesson's flow, with four answers and one right, that checks a Student has grasped an idea rather than that they can do the sums.
_Avoid_: quiz, test (Damodaran's own), check, exam

**after-Lesson check**:
After finishing a Lesson, the owner answers one unseen question for each ability the Lesson promises, without help from the Chatbot. The Lesson passes when the owner demonstrates every ability.
_Avoid_: final exam, Student grade, Test intuition (a question inside the Lesson)

**Note**:
Something a Student saves from a Lesson to come back to later. A Student's Notes are all of these together.
_Avoid_: clipboard, bookmark, favourite

**Class**:
One of Damodaran's own short online classes, which a Topic is built on.
_Avoid_: course (there is one Course), lecture series, Topic (a Topic is ValueIt's)

**Session**:
One video of a Class, with its slide deck and its test.
_Avoid_: lecture, webcast, Lesson (a Lesson is ValueIt's)
