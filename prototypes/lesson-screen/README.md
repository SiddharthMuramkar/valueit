# Try the Lesson screen

Double-click **index.html** in this folder. The prototype opens in your browser, including when you are offline, on **Royal blocks**: the look you chose. It is dark and minimal, royal blue is its one accent, and the amounts are solid blocks you can pick up.

Start by dragging the **bank loan** from 0 to 5,000. The coffee machine and the bank debt appear together and both totals rise to 11,000. The blue block, what is left for Maya, does not move.

The Chatbot has 48% of the screen on the left and the Widget has 52% on the right, the layout you chose earlier.

## What to try in Royal blocks

- **Grab a block.** Pull the blue block up or down to change Maya's own money. Once there is a loan, pull the coffee machine or the bank debt to change it. The blocks that move together are outlined, and a small readout shows the amount and how much it changed. The Cart is grey and does not move.
- **Touch a coloured word** in the Chatbot's text, such as "left for Maya" or "bank loan". Its block, its row under the stacks and its slider light up.
- **Press Notes** at the top right, after saving an answer or a Widget with **Save as Note**.
- **Press Send** to step through the Lesson, or use the dark bar.

## Use the dark bar

**Look** switches designs and keeps your place in the Lesson. Use the left and right arrows. **Moment** steps through the same nine scenes in each look. Use the up and down arrows. While typing or adjusting a slider, the arrow keys operate that control.

Choose **iPad sideways** under **Screen** for a first look. Also try **Small laptop**, which is wider but shorter. Turn on **iPad keyboard up** to see how much space a Student has while typing: the blocks lie down as two bars and, when there is a question, a **Test intuition ↓** link jumps to it.

## The nine moments

Every Widget in the planned product is built live. At **Moment 3**, use **Replay Widget arrival** in the dark bar. **Piece by piece** shows the title and the blocks while the controls are still being built. **All at once** waits and then reveals the whole Widget. The two replays use the same short, simulated build time.

| Moment | What to look for |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Chatbot introduces Maya's coffee cart. Move the loan and compare the two sides. |
| 2 | A Student asks about a machine losing value. The Widget still works during the wait. |
| 3 | The Chatbot builds **The machine wears out**. Drag the years and try switching back to the first Widget. |
| 4 | A question about Starbucks brings a source link under the answer. |
| 5 | The Chatbot resets the loan and opens **Test intuition** inside the Widget. |
| 6 | **I'm stuck** brings a hint and points to the loan control. |
| 7 | A wrong pick prompts the Chatbot to move the loan and explain what changed. |
| 8 | The third answer is right. **Go on** moves the Student forward. |
| 9 | **What to keep** is saved as a Note. Add a line of your own and press **Done**. |

## The other looks

**Look** in the dark bar still holds the three looks Royal blocks was chosen over, and the first rough pass.

| Look | What you see |
|---|---|
| Night glow | A dark instrument panel, mint light and luminous coloured segments. |
| Bold paper | Cream paper, a golden plot and a cobalt block. |
| Playful blocks | Solid coloured blocks on a lilac board. Royal blocks grew out of this one. |
| First rough pass | Three early layouts. A has two panes, B puts words and Widgets into one stream, and C gives the Widget the stage. |

The conversation, money, wait and Student actions are scripted. **Send** advances the scene. The Notes last for the current visit to a look. The Starbucks example demonstrates where a source belongs.
