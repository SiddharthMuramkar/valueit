# Try the Lesson screen

Double-click **index.html** in this folder. The prototype opens in your browser, including when you are offline. Start by dragging the **bank loan** from 0 to 5,000. Watch the coffee machine and the bank debt appear together, then check what happened to the blue amount left for Maya.

The three looks use the layout you chose. The Chatbot has 48% of the screen on the left and the Widget has 52% on the right.

| Look | What you see | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Night glow | A dark instrument panel, mint light and luminous coloured segments. Geist for words and Geist Mono for amounts. | Drag a lit segment or change the loan while the Chatbot is thinking. |
| Bold paper | Cream paper, a golden plot and a cobalt block. Fraunces for headings and totals, DM Sans for conversation. | Pull the bank-loan grip up. Compare the heights with the scale and read the two totals. |
| Playful blocks | Solid coloured blocks with visible edges on a lilac board. Bricolage Grotesque for words and numbers. | Grab the blue block to change Maya’s own contribution, then drag the bank-loan slider and watch the blue block stay put. |

## Use the dark bar

**Look** switches designs and keeps your place in the Lesson. Use the left and right arrows. **Moment** steps through the same nine scenes in each look. Use the up and down arrows. While typing or adjusting a slider, the arrow keys operate that control.

Choose **iPad sideways** under **Screen** for a first comparison. Also try **Small laptop**, which is wider but shorter. Turn on **iPad keyboard up** to see how much space a Student has while typing. You can switch between the Lesson’s Widget and **Built for you**, save a Note, or press the buttons inside the Lesson itself.

## The nine moments

Every Widget in the planned product is built live. At **Moment 3**, use **Replay Widget arrival** in the dark bar. **Piece by piece** shows the title and amounts while the controls are still being built. **All at once** waits and then reveals the whole Widget. The two replays use the same short, simulated build time.

| Moment | What to look for |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Chatbot introduces Maya’s coffee cart. Move the loan and compare the two sides. |
| 2 | A Student asks about a machine losing value. The Widget still works during the wait. |
| 3 | The Chatbot builds **The machine wears out**. Drag the years and try switching back to the first Widget. |
| 4 | A question about Starbucks brings a source link under the answer. |
| 5 | The Chatbot resets the loan and opens **Test intuition** inside the Widget. |
| 6 | **I’m stuck** brings a hint and points to the loan control. |
| 7 | A wrong pick prompts the Chatbot to move the loan and explain what changed. |
| 8 | The third answer is right. **Go on** moves the Student forward. |
| 9 | **What to keep** is saved as a Note. Add a line of your own and press **Done**. |

**First rough pass** in the Look switch still contains the three earlier layouts. A has two panes and a draggable split, B puts words and Widgets into one scrolling stream, and C gives the Widget the stage with the Chatbot underneath.

The conversation, money, wait and Student actions are scripted for this comparison. **Send** advances the scene. The Notes last for the current visit to a look. The Starbucks example demonstrates where a source belongs. This is a throwaway prototype on its own branch.
