# Bold paper

A warm financial broadsheet makes the same sum twice, in large ink numerals over two columns of printed colour. Pull the black grip on the chart or use a slider to see where the money goes.

## Type and colour

Fraunces sets the chapter, Widget titles and totals. DM Sans sets the conversation, controls and ledger. Both variable Latin files ship with their SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `fonts/`. The figures use lining, tabular numerals.

Sizes are CSS pixels. A question moves the title and totals down the scale. With the keyboard up, the message box stays put and the Widget scrolls locally. The question has a shortcut in both directions.

The page uses paper `#fcfaf3`, leaf `#f1eee4`, ink `#20231e`, secondary ink `#52564b`, rules `#b8b9a9` and cobalt accent `#1849b3`. Chart paper is gold `#ffdb82`. Cash uses teal `#16836d`, the cart vermilion `#d44429`, the machine pink `#f38fac`, the bank plum `#7c2545`, and Maya’s share blue `#204ac5`. Opposite hatch directions connect and distinguish the machine and the debt that paid for it.

## Widget kit

- A folio header holds the title and Note button. A second Widget introduces a pair of tabs.
- A sheet takes a maximum, two lists of amounts, and controls from the Lesson’s shared data. The totals, ruled plot, block heights, grip, swatches and ledger values all follow those numbers. The scale stays fixed while a Student drags.
- A native slider has a name, formatted value and keyboard steps. A chart grip drives the primary slider. Adjustable blocks drive their associated slider too.
- A setting from the Chatbot gets a caption at the chart. A hint marks the loan control. New sheets arrive in register, and a change to the holdings moves the affected blocks from their previous positions.
- Test intuition takes the question, answers and selected state. The saved page takes a list of takeaways, a Note button and an editable strip.

## Review notes

The gold chart is most legible at 1180 × 750 or larger. At keyboard height, turn to Test intuition with its shortcut to see all four answers. The source points to the company’s annual filings. The Lesson and its Notes live in memory for this prototype.

The typography guide’s “Use unitless numbers for `line-height`” shaped paragraph spacing. The slider guide’s “Always provide a visible keyboard focus indicator after removing the native appearance” applies to each range. The motion guide’s “Always respect reduced motion preferences” applies to chart assembly, the Chatbot’s changes and the waiting marks.
